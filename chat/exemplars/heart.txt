# A physical system drawn as its parts in their real places (the learner's view: the heart's right side on
# the left), with what flows through it moving; the colour change carries "picks up oxygen".
title "Blood through the heart"
schema plane x=[0,10] y=[0,7] grid=false axes=false
mark lungs rect organ x=5 y=6.2 w=6 h=1.1 label="lungs"
mark ra rect chamber x=3.2 y=4.1 w=2.2 h=1.4 label="right atrium"
mark rv rect chamber x=3.2 y=2.4 w=2.2 h=1.6 label="right ventricle"
mark la rect chamber x=6.8 y=4.1 w=2.2 h=1.4 label="left atrium"
mark lv rect chamber x=6.8 y=2.4 w=2.2 h=1.6 label="left ventricle"
mark body rect organ x=5 y=0.6 w=6 h=0.9 label="the body"
mark blood dot x=1.4 y=0.6 r=9 trail=1.2s color=blue label="blood"
Say: "Used blood comes back from the |body|body into the right atrium."
@body morph blood.x -> 3.2 1s
with morph blood.y -> 4.1 1s
Say: "The right ventricle |pumps|pumps it up to the lungs."
@pumps morph blood.y -> 2.4 0.8s
mark blood y=2.4
morph blood.y -> 6.2 1.2s
Say: "There it picks up |oxygen|oxygen."
@oxygen mark blood color=red
Say: "Back it comes to the |left|left side, and the strong left ventricle sends it out to the body."
@left morph blood.x -> 6.8 1s
with morph blood.y -> 4.1 1s
morph blood.y -> 2.4 0.8s
morph blood.y -> 0.6 1s
Say: "Two pumps side by side: the right one feeds the lungs, the left one feeds everything else."
