# Binary search: pointers on a real array; every look throws half away.
title "Binary search"
mark a array items=[2,5,8,12,16,23,38,56,72] label="sorted, looking for 23"
mark lo label text="lo" at=a[0]
mark hi label text="hi" at=a[8]
Say: "We want twenty-three. The list is sorted, so every look can rule out half of it."
Say: "Check the |m|middle: sixteen."
@m mark mid label text="mid" at=a[4]
with indicate a[4]
Say: "Sixteen is too small, and so is everything left of it. Move lo |past|past it."
@past mark lo at=a[5]
Say: "New middle: |m|thirty-eight. Too big, so hi comes |back|back."
@m mark mid at=a[6]
with indicate a[6]
@back mark hi at=a[5]
Say: "Lo and hi meet on |found|twenty-three. Three looks instead of six."
@found mark mid at=a[5]
with focus a[5]
Say: "Each look halves what's |left|left."
@left mark rule card text="n items take about log₂ n looks" label="Key idea"
with focus none
Say: "Your turn: roughly how many looks would a list of a thousand need?"
