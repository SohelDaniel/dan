# Formulas as things: a live value, terms colored by the thing they name, and a formula that morphs.
title "Kinetic energy"

mark mass bar mass m=2 label="mass" unit=kg
mark speed bar speed v=3 label="speed" unit=m/s
mark ke tex tex="E_k = \frac{1}{2}\textcolor{mass}{m}\textcolor{speed}{v}^2"

Say: "Kinetic energy is one half, times mass, times speed squared."
focus mass
Say: "This is the mass,"
focus speed
Say: "and this is the speed."
focus none
morph ke.tex -> "E_k = \frac{1}{2}\textcolor{mass}{m}\textcolor{speed}{v}^2 = \val{0.5*mass*speed^2}\,\text{J}" 1.5s
Say: "With two kilograms at three metres per second, that's nine joules."
Say: "Now double the |go|speed."
@go morph speed.v 3 -> 6 2s
Say: "Energy goes up four times, because speed is squared."

scene "On a graph"
schema axes x=[0,6] y=[0,40]
mark curve path fn={0.5*2*x^2} label="E(v)"
mark tag tex tex="E = v^2" x=4.2 y=30 size=30
Say: "As a function of speed, the energy is a parabola."
