title "Downhill in two weights"
schema space x=[-3,3] y=[-3,3] z=[0,5]
mark L surface fn={0.4*(x^2 + y^2)} label="loss"
mark p dot x=-2 y=-2 z={0.4*(p.x^2 + p.y^2)} trail=all drop=true label="you"
mark lossval label at=p label="loss" loss={p.z}
mark eq tex tex="\textcolor{p}{z} = 0.4\,(x^2 + y^2)"
mark g vector at=p dx=0.8 dy=0.8 dz=0 label="downhill"
Say: "You start up here, at a loss of {p.z}."
@step morph p.x -> -1 1s
with morph p.y -> -1 1s
Say: "One |step|step downhill, and the loss drops to {p.z}."
@box mark e enclose of=p label="here"
Say: "This |box|point is where you are."
@pulse indicate L
Say: "The whole |pulse|surface is the loss for every pair of weights."
@fly send eq -> p
Say: "The formula |fly|gives its height."
@look focus p
Say: "Keep your eye on |look|the [p]point."
@more morph p.x -> 0 1.2s
with morph p.y -> 0 1.2s
Say: "A few more steps and it reaches the |more|bottom."
hide e
@turn camera orbit=90
Say: "From the side you can |turn|see the bowl."
