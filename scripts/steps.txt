# A derivation is one formula that grows: aligned steps, each new line lit as it arrives, the move annotated.
title "Solving step by step"
mark eq tex tex="\begin{aligned} 2x + \textcolor{three}{3} &= 11 \end{aligned}"
Say: "Start with two x plus three equals eleven."
mark why enclose of=eq[three] label="take it away"
Say: "Take |sub|three from both sides."
@sub morph eq.tex -> "\begin{aligned} 2x + \textcolor{three}{3} &= 11 \\ 2x &= 8 \end{aligned}" 1s
hide why
Say: "Then |half|halve both."
@half morph eq.tex -> "\begin{aligned} 2x + \textcolor{three}{3} &= 11 \\ 2x &= 8 \\ x &= 4 \end{aligned}" 1s
