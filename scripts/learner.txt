# What the learner sets: a param with set=learner gets a slider; dragging it reshapes what it drives.
title "Stretching a parabola"
schema axes x=[-3,3] y=[0,9]
mark k param value=1 range=[0,3] label="stretch k" set=learner
mark f path fn={k*x^2} label="y = k x²"
Say: "Drag the stretch and watch the parabola narrow or widen."
